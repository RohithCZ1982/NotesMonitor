NotesMonitor

A notes distribution platform for educational institutions. Admins organize study materials into date-labeled folders and control access through student groups.

Admin
Student

User Roles

Admin

Full platform control — manages students, groups, folders, and analytics.

Student

Views and downloads materials from folders assigned to their groups.

Getting Started

Admin Login

1
Navigate to /admin/login.
2
Enter the admin email and password. Default credentials are shown below — change them after first login.
3
Click Login. You will be redirected to the Admin Dashboard.

Student Registration

1
Navigate to /login and switch to the Register tab.
2
Fill in the form:
  • Name — your full name (2–100 characters)
  • Mobile — 10-digit Indian number (must start with 6–9)
  • Email — a valid email address
  • Address — optional
  • Password — at least 6 characters
3
Click Register.
Your account is created with Pending status. You cannot view study materials until an admin approves your account.

Student Login

1
Navigate to /login (the Login tab is shown by default).
2
Enter your mobile number and password.
3
Click Login.
If your account is still pending or rejected, you will see a status message on your dashboard instead of folders.

Student Management

Sidebar → Students

Lists all registered students. Filter and search, then approve or reject registrations.

Searching & Filtering

  • Search by name, mobile number, or email.
  • Filter by status: All / Pending / Active / Rejected.
  • Results paginate at 20 per page.

Approving a Student

1
Filter by Pending to find new registrations.
2
Click Approve. Status changes to Active and an email notification is sent.
3
Assign the student to one or more groups so they can access materials.

Rejecting a Student

1
Click Reject next to the student.
2
Status changes to Rejected and an email notification is sent. Rejected students can log in but cannot view folders.

You can change a student's status (Active ↔ Pending ↔ Rejected) at any time.

Group Management

Sidebar → Groups

Groups link students to folders. A student can belong to multiple groups; a folder can be assigned to multiple groups.

Creating a Group

1
Click Create Group.
2
Enter a Name (required) and an optional Description.
3
Click Save.

Managing Students in a Group

  • Click Manage Students on a group row to open the student assignment modal.
  • Select active students and click Add Selected.
  • Remove a student by clicking the remove icon next to their name in the modal.

Editing / Deleting a Group

  • Click the edit icon to update the name or description.
  • Click the delete icon and confirm. Students lose access to folders that were only assigned to the deleted group.

Folder Management

Sidebar → Folders

Folders are the primary containers for study materials. Each folder has a name, a date, files, and assigned groups.

Creating a Folder

1
Click Create Folder.
2
Enter a Folder Name, a Date in YYYY-MM-DD format, and optionally assign groups.
3
Click Save. The folder appears in the grid.

Uploading Files

1
Open a folder and drag-and-drop files onto the upload area, or click to browse.
2
A progress bar shows upload status. Files are stored on Cloudinary CDN.

Supported formats:

JPG PNG WEBP MP4 MOV

Maximum 100 MB per file, up to 20 files per upload batch.

Assigning a Folder to Groups

1
On the folder card, click Assign Groups.
2
Select one or more groups and click Save. Only students in those groups can see the folder.

To make a folder inaccessible, remove all group assignments.

Other Folder Actions

  • Preview — click any image thumbnail to open a full-screen modal; videos preview inline.
  • Download ZIP — click Download ZIP on a folder card to stream all files as a single archive.
  • Delete a file — hover over a thumbnail and click the delete icon. The file is permanently removed from storage.
  • Delete a folder — click the delete icon on the folder card and confirm. All files are permanently deleted from Cloudinary.

Student Features

Student Dashboard

Route: /dashboard

After logging in, students see a grid of all folders they are authorized to view based on their assigned groups.

  • Each card shows the folder name, date, number of images, and number of videos.
  • Use the search bar to filter folders by name.
  • Click a folder card to open it.
If your account is Pending or Rejected, a status message is shown instead of folders. Contact your admin to get approved or re-activated.

Viewing a Folder

Route: /folder/:folderId
  • Images are displayed as a thumbnail grid.
  • Videos are listed below the images.
  • Click any thumbnail or video to open a full-screen preview modal where you can navigate between files.

Downloading Files

Individual File

1
Hover over an image thumbnail or a video item.
2
Click the Download icon. The file downloads directly from Cloudinary.
3
The download is logged for admin analytics.

Entire Folder as ZIP

1
On the Folder View page, click Download All as ZIP.
2
The backend packages all files into a ZIP and streams it to your browser. This download is also tracked.

Profile

Route: /profile
  • View your Name, Mobile, Email, and Address.
  • See your Assigned Groups — this determines which folders are visible to you.
Profile editing is not available from this page. Contact your admin to update your details or group assignment.
